Sale Buddy

Sales, stock, bills, customers, and backups for small shops.

Sale Buddy helps store owners record products, track inventory, create bills, review sales history, manage returns, and keep optional Google Drive app-data backups tied to the user's Google account.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service
Inventory Track products, prices, stock levels, low stock, and out-of-stock items.
Sales and Bills Record sales, customer details, payment status, returns, and bill history.
Backups Use local backups or optional Google Drive app data backups for the signed-in Google account.

Google Drive app data use

Sale Buddy requests Google Drive app data access only for its own hidden backup file. It does not access the user's normal Google Drive files. Users can use Sale Buddy without a Google account and keep data local to the device.
